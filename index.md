# Privacy Policy for Bunko

**Last updated: 25 July 2026**

## The short version

Bunko does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app works entirely offline.

## What stays on your device

Bunko saves a small amount of information locally, on your phone only. It is never sent anywhere:

- **Reading progress** — which screen you reached in each book you have opened.
- **Reading preferences** — furigana and romaji settings, text size, line spacing, and paper theme.
- **Purchase state** — whether the full library has been unlocked.

This data is stored in your device's app storage. Uninstalling Bunko removes all of it.

## Purchases

Bunko offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks the full library permanently. Payment is handled entirely by **Google Play**. Bunko never sees or stores your payment details. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/intl/en/about/play-terms/) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Text-to-speech

If you tap the speaker icon on a word or kana, Bunko asks your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce it. This uses the speech service already installed on your phone. Depending on your device settings, that service may be provided by Google or your handset manufacturer, and its behaviour is governed by their policies. Bunko sends only the single word or phrase being pronounced, and stores nothing.

## Network access

Bunko does not require an internet connection to read any book. All story text, illustrations, and fonts are bundled in the app itself. The only network activity is the Google Play billing check when you open the unlock screen or restore a purchase.

## Children

Bunko does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
